LMMSE Equalization and Practical Design

Regularization trades a little residual ISI for much less noise enhancement

ZF tries to invert every channel notch. LMMSE includes N₀I, limits extreme gain, and moves from inverse filtering at high SNR toward matched filtering at low SNR.
