Fixed-weight spatial repetition: one effective channel

When the same symbol is transmitted simultaneously from two antennas with fixed equal-power weights and no channel knowledge at the transmitter, the two contributions form one effective complex channel. Depending on their relative phase, they can add constructively or destructively.

Simultaneous equal-power spatial repetition
heff = (h₁ + h₂)/√2
γrep = (GEx/N₀)|heff|²

The √2 normalisation keeps the total transmitted symbol energy equal to Ex. “Generate a Rayleigh-fading channel” generates independent, zero-mean, unit-variance circularly symmetric complex Gaussian coefficients, denoted CN(0,1), and rounds their displayed magnitudes and phases to the slider resolution.
