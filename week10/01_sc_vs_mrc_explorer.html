Selection Combining and Maximal-Ratio Combining

For independent, identically distributed Rayleigh-fading branches with equal average signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) and perfect channel knowledge at the receiver, selection combining (SC) uses the branch with the largest instantaneous SNR. Maximal-ratio combining (MRC) aligns the branch phases and weights each branch in proportion to its channel magnitude.
