Zero-forcing noise enhancement

Zero-forcing (ZF) detection inverts the channel to remove interference between spatial streams. As the two channel columns become more similar, the smaller singular value approaches zero; the inverse then applies a large gain in that channel direction and amplifies the receiver noise.

Channel

H = [ 1   ρ ;   ρ   1 ]

ρ is a matrix coefficient, not the normalised correlation between the two channel columns. The normalised column correlation is displayed below.
