Singular-value decomposition eigenmodes and rank adaptation

The singular-value decomposition (SVD) represents the channel as H = UΣVH. The right singular vectors in V steer transmitted symbols into mutually orthogonal channel directions, and the receiver projects onto the left singular vectors in U. This produces parallel scalar channels with gains σ₁ and σ₂. Rank adaptation selects how many strong modes carry data.
